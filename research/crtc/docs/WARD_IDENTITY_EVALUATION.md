# Is the CRTC defect a Ward identity?

Not on the evidence currently available.

## Exact identity that is proved

The repository proves the response identity

`Delta/|Z|^2 = 1-x0 Re(1/Z)`.

This is a Möbius/inverse-response identity.  It turns circle membership into a rigidity condition on the real part of the measured inverse response.

C6 symmetry separately proves that an isolated real two-dimensional shear block has the form

`aI+bJ`.

These two facts explain why the geometry is so simple, but neither fact enforces `a(p)=a0`.

## What “Ward identity” normally means here

In linear response, a Ward identity is generated by a symmetry/conservation law and relates response functions or their derivatives.  Bradlyn, Goldstein and Read derive viscosity Kubo formulas and Ward identities from strain generators and, with additional assumptions such as Galilean invariance, relate stress response to conductivity.

Calling the CRTC defect formula a Ward identity before identifying the symmetry/conservation law that forces the rigid condition would overstate the result.

## Precise Ward-like criterion

At renormalization scale `ell`, define

`G_ell(p)=Re[1/Z_ell(p)]-Re[1/Z_ell(p0)]`.

CRTC is the constraint `G_ell(p)=0`.  A genuine protection principle would have to force the flow to remain tangent to this constraint surface:

`partial_ell G_ell(p)=0` whenever `G_ell(p)=0`.

In kernel coordinates this is

`beta_a(p)-beta_a(p0)=0`.

If a microscopic symmetry or conservation law can be shown to imply that relation, then the CRTC rigidity condition would acquire a genuine Ward-identity interpretation.  The current repository deliberately leaves that as an open model-specific problem rather than naming the algebraic defect itself a Ward identity.
