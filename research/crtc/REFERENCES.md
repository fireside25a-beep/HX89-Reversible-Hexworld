# Primary references

1. A. A. Allocca, S. Heidari, T. Iadecola, A. Rahmani, P. Ghaemi, S. Ganeshan, **Odd fluids from chiral cellular automata**, arXiv:2606.19431 (2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19431
   - Chiral FHP collision model, linearized complex shear eigenvalue, and analytical shear/Hall viscosities used for the exact Boltzmann check.

2. C. Hargus, K. Klymko, J. M. Epstein, K. K. Mandadapu, **Time reversal symmetry breaking and odd viscosity in active fluids: Green-Kubo and NEMD results**, J. Chem. Phys. 152, 201102 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0006441
   - Green-Kubo relations for odd/shear viscosity tested against nonequilibrium molecular dynamics.

3. J. M. Epstein, K. K. Mandadapu, **Time-reversal symmetry breaking in two-dimensional nonequilibrium viscous fluids**, Phys. Rev. E 101, 052614 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.101.052614
   - General isotropic 2D nonequilibrium viscous constitutive structure and Green-Kubo framework.

4. D. Banerjee, A. Souslov, A. G. Abanov, V. Vitelli, **Odd viscosity in chiral active fluids**, Nature Communications 8, 1573 (2017). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-01378-7
   - Odd viscosity as a reactive/nondissipative coefficient in isotropic chiral active fluids.

5. M. Fruchart, M. Han, C. Scheibner, V. Vitelli, **The odd ideal gas: Hall viscosity and thermal conductivity from non-Hermitian kinetic theory**, arXiv:2202.02037 (2022). https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.02037
   - Non-Hermitian collision operators and microscopic origin of odd transport.

6. B. Bradlyn, M. Goldstein, N. Read, **Kubo formulas for viscosity: Hall viscosity, Ward identities, and the relation with conductivity**, Phys. Rev. B 86, 245309 (2012). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.86.245309
   - Correct use of Kubo formulas and genuine Ward identities for viscosity response.

7. T. Naitoh, M. H. Ernst, J. W. Dufty, **Long-time tails in two-dimensional cellular automata fluids**, Phys. Rev. A 42, 7187 (1990). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.42.7187
   - Long-time tails in the FHP cellular automaton; relevant to the existence of infinite-time 2D Green-Kubo transport coefficients.

8. U. Frisch, B. Hasslacher, Y. Pomeau, **Lattice-Gas Automata for the Navier-Stokes Equation**, Phys. Rev. Lett. 56, 1505 (1986). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.56.1505
   - Foundational FHP hydrodynamic lattice-gas result.
